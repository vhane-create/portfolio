# Healthcare Bookkeeping & Month-End Close
## Management brief
Vannetta Haney | Enterprise healthcare finance portfolio | November 2026 | USD

Meridian Care Network is fictional. All financial data, documents and population figures are simulated. This project was directed by Vannetta Haney and developed with Codex assistance. It demonstrates a reviewable bookkeeping workflow; it is not a claim of past employer results.

## Network and scope
The case models 480 locations in four regions, 5,000 employees, 2.4 million annual unique patients, 300,000 November unique patients and 400,000 November encounters. These are distinct population assumptions, not a patient-level database. A person can have several encounters. Annual unique patients cannot be computed by adding monthly unique counts. Financial records are summarized posting batches and four aggregate receivable pools. No real health or patient data is used.

## Revenue and collection treatment
Insurance services are billed at $70M with a modeled allowed amount of $45M. The $25M contractual reduction is recorded as contra-revenue and a credit to receivables. Patient services add $20M. Total recognized net service revenue is $65M. Contractual reductions are distinct from expected credit losses.

Opening gross receivables are $18M. Add $90M in gross charges, subtract $25M contractual reductions and $57M collections to obtain $26M ending gross receivables. Collections comprise $15M from opening receivables and $42M from November services. Of ending receivables, $15M is current, $8M is 1–30 days overdue and $3M is 31–60 days overdue. Case-specific loss rates of 2%, 10% and 40% produce a $2.3M allowance. These are illustrative estimates, not benchmarks.

## Why cash differs from income
Start with $21.55M income. Add $1M depreciation and $2.3M provision for credit losses. Subtract $8M growth in gross receivables, add $0.8M supplies reduction, subtract $5M prepaid insurance growth, subtract $1M decline in accounts payable and add $3.5M payroll accrual. Operating cash is $15.15M.

After $6M equipment spending, $2M principal repayment and $2M owner distributions, cash increases $5.15M from its $40M opening balance. The bank statement ends at $44.65M. Add $3M deposits in transit and subtract $2.5M outstanding payments to reconcile to $45.15M. Book cash starts at $45.2M before a $75,000 fee and $25,000 interest receipt.

## Management actions
1. Reconcile the $25M contractual adjustment to the simulated payer terms before approving net revenue. Omitting it overstates both profit and receivables by $25M while the trial balance still balances.
2. Prioritize the $3M overdue patient pool and evaluate the $1.2M loss estimate against collection evidence. Patient pool identifiers are aggregate case labels.
3. Reserve liquidity for $3.5M of accrued payroll and validate payment timing. Omitting the accrual overstates profit and understates liabilities by $3.5M without changing cash.
4. Verify that deposits and outstanding payments clear in December. Do not post bank timing differences a second time.
5. Review the $5M increase in prepaid insurance as a cash planning item. November expense is $1M across two $6M annual policies.

## Assumptions and limits
The opening $6M prepaid balance is treated as an unexpired annual policy at November 1. A second $6M policy begins November 1. Each incurs $0.5M monthly expense. The $1M depreciation schedule is supplied aggregate evidence and includes the month's additions. No separate asset-level useful-life model is claimed.

This is one consolidated, accrual-basis month, before income tax. It excludes patient-level claims adjudication, refunds, deferred revenue, inventory costing beyond supplies, tax accounting, legal entity consolidation and regulatory submissions. Interest paid and received are classified as operating cash flows for this case. Debt principal and distributions are financing; equipment purchases are investing. The adjusted trial balance is pre-closing. Current income and distributions flow into equity in the financial statement presentation.

All amounts are USD. The active web scenario changes estimates and selected posting completeness. Source-register and downloadable baseline CSVs remain the baseline, while the workspace export buttons produce the active scenario. Source evidence and estimated loss rates remain fixed unless the case explicitly exposes an input. Close status reflects posting completeness and arithmetic controls; human preparer and reviewer approvals remain blank.
