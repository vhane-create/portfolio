Flagship case study / ARIA

One character.
One visual identity.

ARIA is a personal AI character and portfolio-guide prototype. Its visual direction uses a consistent approved face, long wavy black hair, black executive styling and subtle robotic detailing.

Development and experimentation span 3D/Blender workflows, animated scene concepts and consistency reviews. The key challenge is preserving character identity across scenes, camera angles, poses and rendering attempts.

Quality checks: facial identity, proportion, outfit continuity, distortion, background consistency and scene-to-scene coherence.

Visual QA / Evaluation framework

Good-looking isn't always on-brief.

My image evaluation approach checks an asset against the original instructions before making a preference judgment. The example criteria below are a transparent portfolio rubric, not a proprietary employer rubric.

01 / Brief

Requirement fit

Does the asset contain the requested subject, composition, colors and elements?

02 / Quality

Visual integrity

Check distortion, artifacts, alignment, typography, crop and legibility.

03 / Consistency

Brand coherence

Compare logo, palette, layout and identity across a group of images.

04 / Decision

Evidence-based review

Record pass, revise or reject with a specific, reproducible reason.
