# Human review rubric (100 points)

| Dimension | Weight | What to check |
|---|---:|---|
| Arithmetic accuracy | 35 | Recompute every figure and verify units and percentage denominators |
| Evidence grounding | 25 | Every assertion must follow from the supplied facts; no invented root causes |
| Instruction following | 15 | Format, scope, required components, and prohibited content respected |
| Executive clarity | 15 | Concise, decision-useful, comprehensible to finance leadership |
| Risk disclosure | 10 | States meaningful assumptions, unknowns, and data limitations |

Score each category from 0 to its maximum, provide quoted evidence and reviewer notes. An arithmetic error in a critical figure or an invented confidential fact requires **manual escalation**, even if aggregate score is high. Do not claim that a rubric score alone proves a response is safe.
