Model design and review controls

Revenue engine

Average quarterly customers × annual contract value / 4. Quarterly customer growth and annual price changes compound.

Workforce planning

Average quarterly headcount × annual loaded salary / 4. Quarterly additions increase the cost base.

Operating model

Revenue minus cost of service, payroll and other OpEx equals EBITDA. Expenses are modeled separately.

Cash conversion

FCF = EBITDA less modeled cash taxes, working-capital investment and capex. Simplified proxy, not a GAAP cash flow statement.
