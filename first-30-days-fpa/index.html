First 30 Days: FP&A Delivery Plan

How I would establish financial reporting confidence, operating visibility and decision support.

This is a proposed onboarding approach, not a promise of access, a guaranteed timeline or a claim about previous employer results. Actual priorities depend on the role, systems, stakeholders, reporting calendar and management approval.

Days 1–7 · Understand & validate

  • Align on CFO/finance leadership priorities and reporting calendar.
  • Map data sources, chart of accounts, forecast owners and key controls.
  • Review actuals, budget assumptions, material variances and known risks.
  • Document data gaps and agree on an initial reporting baseline.
Potential deliverable: source inventory, stakeholder map and reconciled baseline.

Related example: Controls Lab

Days 8–15 · Improve visibility

  • Prepare a repeatable budget-versus-actual reporting template.
  • Define variance materiality and review/approval steps.
  • Automate safe, repeatable data transformations where appropriate.
  • Draft a concise management narrative with clearly sourced numbers.
Potential deliverable: reviewed variance package and QA checklist.

Related example: Delivery Lab

Days 16–23 · Forecast & challenge

  • Validate forecast drivers with business partners.
  • Model upside, base and downside operating assumptions.
  • Evaluate cash, hiring and cost implications.
  • Surface decisions, risks and owners for leadership review.
Potential deliverable: scenario forecast and decision log.

Related example: Forecast Model

Days 24–30 · Deliver & hand off

  • Present an executive-ready financial review and open risks.
  • Confirm reconciliations and documentation for reproducibility.
  • Recommend a practical reporting cadence and ownership model.
  • Agree on next-quarter improvement priorities and success measures.
Potential deliverable: leadership briefing, documented controls and 60-day roadmap.

Related example: CFO Suite

Measures I would propose tracking

On-time reporting delivery, reconciled balances, unresolved exceptions, forecast variance, review turnaround, and the number of manual reporting steps eliminated. Baselines and targets should be agreed with the team; no performance percentages are assumed.
