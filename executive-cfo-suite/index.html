    Risks and limitations

    Revenue and costs are modeled independently. COGS is held constant in this simplified sensitivity exercise, so this is not a fully integrated three-statement forecast. Validate pricing, sales volume, cost behavior, and workforce assumptions before any real decision. EBITDA excludes depreciation, interest, taxes, and non-operating items.

    Model transparency and reproducibility

    EBITDA = Revenue − COGS − Payroll − Other operating costs. Scenario revenue = $114m × (1 + revenue change). Payroll = $34m × (1 + payroll change). Other operating costs = $16m × (1 + operating cost change). Variance = Scenario − Budget for revenue/EBITDA; cost variances are shown as scenario cost less budget cost. The operating bridge reconciles the EBITDA variance using revenue variance less each cost variance.
