Manufacturing costs and FIFO margin

What the costing schedule explains

200 units are produced using $9,000 of materials, $6,000 of direct labor and $5,000 of applied overhead. Unit production cost is $100.

230 units sell for $200 each. FIFO consumes 100 opening units at $80 and 130 current units at $100. The remaining 70 units carry a $7,000 inventory balance.

Actual overhead is $5,500. The $500 underapplied amount is treated as immaterial and charged to cost of goods sold at close. This case assumption is disclosed rather than presented as a universal treatment.
