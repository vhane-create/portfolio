Selected case studies / Brief · Role · Deliverables · Results

Design decisions in context

Real business and personal projects are labeled separately from fictional portfolio studies. Outcomes are limited to what can be documented.

Real business / Beautiunlimited Salon & Spa LLC

Storefront identity & service presentation

Brief: Present a recognizable salon-and-spa identity to people passing the storefront and display examples of hair services.

My role: Business owner overseeing how the salon is represented and how services are presented. Specific fabrication or graphic-design credits have not been independently established.

Deliverables: An installed black-and-gold storefront identity with a floral profile graphic and a window display of hairstyle photographs, documented in a storefront photograph.

Results: The branding was installed and visible at the operating business. Customer conversion or revenue impact has not been measured here.

Personal project / ARIA

3D character identity & continuity

Brief: Develop a recognizable AI portfolio guide whose appearance stays consistent across scenes and presentations.

My role: Project direction, visual-reference selection, Blender/3D experimentation and visual review.

Deliverables: Approved character reference, prototype scene concepts and visual-quality criteria.

Results: A consistent approved reference and a public prototype presentation; ongoing work on scene and motion consistency.

Fictional design studies / AUREL · VOLTERRA · FORME

Brand identities across audiences

Brief: Explore contrasting visual languages for wellness, immersive technology and editorial products.

My role: Portfolio concept direction and evaluation of identity treatments.

Deliverables: Three fictional logo directions, palettes, visual rationales and an interactive identity preview.

Results: Three distinct, publicly viewable concepts with working brand and layout controls; no client or commercial performance claimed.

Supporting evidence: the Beautiunlimited storefront photograph was supplied by the business owner for this case study. The photo is not yet embedded on this site.

Design rationale

01 / AUREL

Botanical Spa

Evergreen, gold and soft editorial styling. A calm premium identity for wellness packaging, service menus and booking experiences.

02 / VOLTERRA

Immersive Technology

Electric violet and cyan with geometric energy. A flexible fictional identity for digital products and immersive experiences.

03 / FORME

Objects & Spaces

Warm neutrals, terracotta and structured type. An editorial identity for objects, interiors and product storytelling.

How the designs are evaluated

Each concept can be reviewed for brief adherence, palette consistency, typography legibility, visual hierarchy, layout flexibility and contrast. The interactive treatments demonstrate how a design system responds to changes without losing its identity.
