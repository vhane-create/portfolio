# Integrated Senior FP&A Forecast Workbook — Portfolio Case Study

A complete **11-tab, formula-linked Excel workbook** has been created as a personal finance simulation.

## Workbook tabs
1. Read Me — model purpose and instructions
2. Assumptions — customers, churn, pricing, costs, hiring, capex, tax, working capital
3. Revenue — customer bridge, average customers, revenue, gross profit
4. Headcount — employee bridge and salary expense
5. OpEx — operating expense drivers
6. P&L Forecast — gross profit, EBITDA, EBIT, net income, margins
7. Cash Flow — net income, depreciation, working capital, capex and ending cash
8. Balance Sheet — cash, receivables, net PP&E, payables, equity and balance check
9. Scenarios — adjustable revenue, service cost and payroll shocks
10. Variance — illustrative budget vs actual comparison
11. Executive Dashboard — linked executive KPIs and checks

**Important:** The Excel workbook was generated as a downloadable chat artifact and is **not yet hosted in this GitHub repository**. Do not present this documentation as a working download link. Once the binary workbook is uploaded, add a relative link here.

## Model methodology
Revenue uses average opening/ending customers times annual price. Cost of service is a percentage of revenue. Payroll uses average headcount times loaded salary. Operating cash flow adjusts net income for depreciation, receivables and payables. Capex reduces cash and increases net PP&E. The balance-sheet check should equal zero under the initial funding assumptions.

## Skills demonstrated
Senior FP&A, integrated financial modeling, scenario analysis, working capital, three-statement planning, driver-based forecasts, budget vs actuals, management reporting and model auditing.

All data is **simulated**. This project does not contain actual employer, client or salon financial information.
