# Flagship ARIA Complete Creation

**Amara Renée Imani Alexandria — ARIA for short.** Her nickname comes from her initials: **A**mara **R**enée **I**mani **A**lexandria. She is an AI model prototype created by **Vannetta Haney**.

Creator and vision: Vannetta Haney (Netta).

## Separate project, preserved introduction

This is ARIA's own flagship, distinct from her role introducing Netta's portfolio. Preserve the existing introduction, full tour, approved source clips, working features and previous review versions. Do not replace the portfolio opening with this flagship.

## 5-10-year simulation vision

A rigged 3D ARIA rendered inside a simulated world, viewed on a simulation screen or monitor. The long-term system should support different views of her body, coherent movement between locations, natural conversation and eventually hours of interaction. A video player is a presentation foundation and visual reference, not proof of a 3D implementation.

## Approved identity and behavior

- Preserve the approved face, skin tone, slender proportions, robotic neck details and Heart (af_heart) voice.
- Warm and welcoming: relaxed eyes, ordinary blinking, a gentle smile and modest natural mouth/jaw movements. Avoid staring eyes, exaggerated mouth openings, stern or dismissive delivery, and sharp theatrical gestures.
- Current baseline: long wavy black hair and black executive suit. Develop additional suits, casual clothes and occasional hairstyles while keeping her recognizable.

## Work streams

1. Full-screen presence: ARIA alone, head to shoes, with optional unobtrusive controls.
2. Speech: microphone input, relevant responses, follow-up context, Heart speech and audio-driven facial motion. Early 2027 is a prototype target, subject to working services and testing.
3. Individuality: consistent style, mannerisms, humor and approved preferences.
4. Simulation: rigged body, conversational movement, sitting, standing and walking; office, home, outdoor and park environments.
5. Wardrobe and hair: continuity, multiple outfits and controlled style changes.
6. Transportation: ARIA's virtual car, approach/entry/riding scenes and eventually driving scenes.
7. Extended interaction: interruptions, reconnects, session boundaries and endurance testing for hours of use.

The portfolio presentation retains a separate maximum five-minute Q&A window. Longer flagship sessions are a later development track.

## First milestone

A dedicated full-screen standing preview built from approved footage, a separate portfolio entry and this charter. Natural-expression revisions are review candidates. Live synchronized conversation, a rigged 3D ARIA, changing environments, new outfits and hours of interaction must not be marked complete until individually demonstrated.
