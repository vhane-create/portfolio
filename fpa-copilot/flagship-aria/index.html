Flagship ARIA Complete Creation

Artificial Reasoning Intelligence Assistant. Concept and vision: Vannetta Haney (Netta).

This is ARIA's own full-screen space. The complete approved standing performance plays once with Heart narration. It is a recorded preview; it does not provide live conversation or hours of generated interaction.

The development horizon is 5-10 years. The next priority is a presentable tour and a maximum five-minute question session. A conversational prototype is the early-2027 target, subject to integration and testing.

Planned: a consistent personality, multiple suits and casual outfits, occasional hairstyle changes, home and outdoor settings, park walks, her own car, and longer conversations. Her approved facial identity and Heart voice remain the baseline. The long-term goal is a rigged 3D simulation with interactive environments, viewed through a monitor. Existing video assets remain part of the introduction and visual reference archive.
