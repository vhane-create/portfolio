Interview-ready explanation

“My portfolio contains reproducible FP&A case studies. I can walk through the business question, model assumptions, calculations, validation checks, executive interpretation and tradeoffs. The data is illustrative, so the demonstrations show my approach and technical capability rather than representing an employer's financial results.”

Financial models are illustrative and intentionally scoped. Enterprise deployments require additional accounting-policy, access-control, data-integration, review and audit procedures.
