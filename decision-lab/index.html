Financial modeling methodology

Incremental sales × contribution margin less fixed operating cost determines EBITDA. Straight-line depreciation of initial investment over five years is used to estimate taxable operating income. Taxes are floored at zero; loss carryforwards are not modeled.

Unlevered free cash flow = EBITDA − modeled cash taxes − investment in incremental working capital. Year 0 includes initial investment; Year 5 releases working capital. NPV discounts cash flows at WACC; IRR is computed by bisection where a root exists.

Excluded: financing, debt tax shields, salvage value, inflation segmentation, capacity limits, noncash deferred tax and tax loss carryforwards. Not GAAP statements or investment advice.
