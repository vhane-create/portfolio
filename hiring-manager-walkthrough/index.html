90-Second Hiring Manager Walkthrough

What I can deliver in FP&A—and how to evaluate it quickly.

Evidence policy: The linked models are portfolio simulations, not employer records. Timing claims are limited to measurements made during actual demo runs. This is a suggested presentation script, not a recorded video.

0:00–0:20 · Financial insight

Start with the business decision

“I help finance teams turn operating data into reliable forecasts and decisions. Here is my Executive CFO Reporting Suite, where financial assumptions flow into variance analysis and management recommendations.”

0:20–0:40 · Financial modeling

Demonstrate connected statements

“In my Three-Statement Forecast, revenue, expenses, working capital, financing and cash are linked. You can change the assumptions and inspect the balance sheet check.”

0:40–1:00 · Speed and accuracy

Show the reporting workflow

“My Finance Delivery Performance Lab runs a synthetic reporting batch, measures processing time in the browser and verifies reconciliation checks before exporting a summary.”

1:00–1:20 · Business partnership

Show operating impact

“The Workforce Planning Lab connects hiring timing, compensation, attrition and benefits to a labor budget. The Treasury Lab demonstrates liquidity sensitivity.”

1:20–1:30 · Close

Make the next step easy

“I can walk through the assumptions, calculations, controls and recommendations in any of these projects. I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss the reporting or planning challenges your finance team needs solved.”
