Controls and decision notes

Method: revenue = plan × (1 + demand change) × (1 + price change). Variable costs flex with revenue; fixed costs and payroll are modeled separately. EBITDA excludes depreciation, interest and taxes. Cash rolls forward using EBITDA less cash tax, capex, debt service and an assumed working-capital investment. No financing facility is assumed. Annual values are split into four equal quarters for this illustrative outlook.
