Controls and decision notes

Method: monthly revenue shares Jan–Dec = 6%, 6%, 7%, 7%, 8%, 8%, 8%, 8%, 9%, 10%, 11%, 12%. Fixed costs, recurring capex and debt service are spread evenly. Payroll uses starting FTE plus hires from their start month, with salary and burden. Opening operating working capital is an explicit balance; subsequent month-end balances equal that month’s revenue × WC ratio. Project initial working capital is funded with its investment. Monthly cash tax = max(EBITDA, 0) × tax rate; this simplified cash proxy excludes depreciation and loss carryforwards. Net cash flow includes debt service. No new financing is assumed. Investment valuation uses the existing five-year NPV/IRR project at a 12% hurdle, scaled to investment size and demand/price; its future returns begin after this forecast year. Imported report periods map to quarter-close months in this illustrative planning year. This is a planning simulation, not a full three-statement forecast.
